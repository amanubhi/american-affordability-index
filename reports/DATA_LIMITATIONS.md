# Limitations of implemented model v1

- **Selected essentials:** rent, food at home and regular gasoline only. Healthcare, childcare, utilities, taxes, debt, insurance, savings and other expenses are omitted. This is not an official cost-of-living or welfare index.
- **Illustrative weights:** $12,000/$4,800/$1,800 annual 2015 component budgets are assumptions. The model compares these modeled costs with changing aggregate renter income medians; it does not track actual expenditure or the same households. Income is before-tax household money income, not wages or disposable income.
- **Geography:** primary price series are national. The state product measures housing/income only. Statewide medians hide within-state/local and household-type differences. DC is separate; Puerto Rico/other territories are outside the first release.
- **Housing definitions:** the national model uses rent CPI and omits utilities. State gross rent includes applicable utilities and is a paid-rent level estimate. Asking rent, home prices and mortgage affordability are different concepts and excluded.
- **Time:** full standard ACS years are 2015–2019 and 2021–2024. Experimental 2020 is not inserted; five-year ACS is not substituted. Annual affordability stops at 2024. Prices extend through September 2026 (Kaggle through July); partial years do not become full-year affordability figures.
- **Missing CPI:** October 2025 is unavailable for the three CPI series. Complete-period rules produce null 2025 annual prices. Monthly changes also remain missing immediately after an unavailable month. No outliers or missing records are silently removed/interpolated.
- **Uncertainty:** ACS estimates carry published 90% MOEs. The dashboard exposes dollar-input MOEs and not-computed burden exclusions, but does not estimate confidence intervals for aggregate-median ratios, ranks or derived burden shares. Close ranks may not be distinct.
- **Index versus price:** CPI measures price change; average-price series may also reflect sample/product mix and are not official inflation indexes. Units differ across raw items. Review corrections retain source categories. Overlapping broad/narrow meat series are unsuitable as additive quantities; category medians are unweighted descriptive summaries, not spending contributions.
- **Relationships:** levels, monthly changes, detrending, seasonality and period checks can give different results. Shared deflation can itself induce association. Lag searches and rolling correlations are descriptive, with serial dependence and multiple comparisons. No causal effect or forecast performance is established.
- **Scenarios:** historical quantiles are chosen assumptions, applied jointly, not probabilities or confidence bounds. Terminal annualized costs differ from total next-12-month outlay, which assumes gradual monthly compounding. No ML forecast was justified or fitted.
- **Validation limits:** all selected ACS values and source keys were checked structurally and burden universes reconcile; this is not an independent Census survey validation. Kaggle matches its wide file and a BLS egg spot-check, not a complete reconstruction of the compiler's extraction. The frozen snapshot can differ from later revisions.
- **Delivery verification:** Streamlit application tests and browser-companion arithmetic tests pass. Charts and PDF pages were rendered and inspected. Live browser preview was declined by the browser approval gate, so responsive layout/map rendering in a real browser remains unverified in this session. The public companion runs client-side; Streamlit source is included for local/Community Cloud use.

See the source registry, raw manifests, model definitions and validation report for exact provenance and coverage. No source was selected solely on a Kaggle discovery link. Zillow and CE numerical inputs are not part of this release.
